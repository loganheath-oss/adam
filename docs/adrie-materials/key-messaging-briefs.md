## Sprint 10 THEME: Outcome Alignment
Highlight specific project outcomes with freelancers.
Theme Hypothesis:
IF we can align our creative with new Outcome pages
THEN we will build on Upwork research and tap into clients' needs
BECAUSE past sprints show specific beats generic (projects).
What We're Testing:
- Highlighting specific projects from the new Outcomes pages
- Top performing ad creative formats + testing new formats
Angle: Businesses need to know what Upwork can do for them.
Why: Upwork's brand perception is low. Answer clients' "what" and "why" questions quickly.
Six on-site outcomes: Launch a high-converting site · Scale your paid ads · Build chatbot
support · Build a standout brand · Workflow automation · Build a sales pipeline
The six outcomes closely map to the top performing L1 categories. Instead of job titles,
copy & creative will focus on projects and potential outcomes.
Ad Copy: Pull from the Sprint 9 examples · Pull from customer testimonials · Pull from the
landing page copy · Lean into "faster than competitors" language (ex. Build a strong sales
pipeline faster than your competition).

## Sprint 1 Theme: Your Expert > Marketplace
Copy direction: Position Upwork as the place to find one perfect expert will outperform
marketplace scale messaging.
Theme Hypothesis:
IF we highlight Upwork's quality talent and ability to match clients & freelancers
THEN we will attract high-intent SMBs looking for higher end contracts
BECAUSE companies need the right talent now, not thousands of options.
What We're Testing:
- Messaging that highlights Upwork's high quality experts
- Messaging that highlights business outcomes (speed, scaling, ROI), instead of platform features
- Testing messaging that highlights "fractional" job titles
- Visual treatments inspired by prior winners but stylistically evolved for algorithm differentiation
Angle: Find your perfect match vs. all the hiring options
Why: We've learned "specialized experts" is a key performance-driving message and are
goaled with driving more high-intent SMBs (vs. one-off projects).
RESOURCES: Top performing past "Quality Talent" messaging:
"Myth: Freelancers can't handle complex, high-stakes work. Truth: Leading companies rely on
Upwork to connect with skilled professionals who deliver on what matters. ✔︎ Specialized
experts across industries ✔︎ Flexible support for urgent and ongoing work ✔︎ Trusted by
global teams. Important work gets done here—on Upwork."
"Your proven specialist is just a few clicks away. Post your job and get started today."
Top headlines: Start hiring smarter · Hire like a pro · Come back and find AI experts —
fast. · Get started for free. · Post a job for free.
Top creatives feature: Humans (quality talent = humans at the center) · Carousels with job
titles · Comparison grids (Myth vs. Truth) · Light-weight motion GIFs

## Theme #2: Meet the Moment v2
Speed & cost are consistent winning messages.
Hypothesis: IF we position Upwork as a trusted place to save on labor costs THEN we will
increase conversions amongst scaling SMBs BECAUSE companies need to control spending and
onboard talent fast.
What We're Testing:
- Messaging that highlights SMB business outcomes (savings, speed to hire the right talent)
- Messaging that highlights Upwork as a trusted hiring partner/marketplace
- NO market-conditions messaging (no tariff/war mentions), NO fear-mongering (performed poorly MTM'25)
- Visual treatments inspired by prior winners but stylistically evolved
Angle: Upwork is a trusted hiring solution. Hiring is within a SMB's control.
Why: Speed & cost are consistent messaging winners in Meta creative; business costs rising.
Top past messaging: "In a time when every dollar counts, Upwork makes it easier to hire top
freelancers on your terms and only pay for work you need." · "Tighten spending without
slowing down..." · "Top talent doesn't have to break your budget..." (💼 Skilled pros at the
right price point ⚡ Scale fast without the full-time cost ✅ Quality work without compromise)
Top creatives: AI-generated GIFs (Cut Costs) · Client testimonials · Text-only "Here's how
Upwork can help".

## Sprint 7: Past Winners in Different Styles
Re-use exact on-creative ad copy, using a different top-performing ad style.
IF we re-use proven past winning ad copy with a different creative style THEN Meta's
algorithm will recognize the image as new creative BECAUSE the visual style will be
meaningfully different than the original.
Top headlines: Cut hidden hiring costs · Top firms rely on freelancers · Scale up or down
fast · Unlock worldwide talent · Ready for Gen Z talent?

## Sprint 8: Myths/Truths v2
Build on Myth/Truth performance with additional SMB freelance hiring myths.
IF we can "bust" myths SMBs have hiring freelancers THEN we will increase conversions
amongst our core SMB audience BECAUSE Myths/Truths ad style is a new, scaling ad format.
Example Myths/Truths: "Myth: You need a big team to do big work. Truth: $3.3 billion in
complex, strategic projects have been completed on Upwork." · "Myth: Hiring a freelancer
takes weeks. Truth: Post today, get proposals within 24 hours."
Potential styles: Text Only static · Text Only carousel · ChatBubble video · Post It Note static.
