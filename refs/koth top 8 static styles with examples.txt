Key Takeaways
What's winning: Testimonial and PersonWithTextInCorner are the clear top performers, both delivering 10%+
better $/JP than the static average. Testimonials work especially well with black backgrounds and real freelancer
quotes.
AdStudio-Inspired is a pattern: Many of the top performers across PersonWithTextInCorner,
TopFreelancerProfile, and TextWithFaces carry the "HT-ADSTUDIO-INSPIRED" tag, suggesting this creative
approach consistently outperforms.
Black backgrounds dominate: Across all 8 styles, dark/black backgrounds appear in the majority of top-
performing ads. StrongGlimmer also performs well where used.
Undertested styles with potential: ChatBubble (1 ad), TextWithButtonCursor (2 ads), and Carousel (1 ad) all beat
the static average but have very small sample sizes. These are strong candidates for expanded testing in future
sprints.
Note: HIDDEN-HIRING-COSTS was originally tagged as STL:GraphicWithText in the ad name but has been
reclassified here as PersonWithTextInCorner based on the actual creative visual. With that reclassification,
GraphicWithText drops to 1 ad (COSTFLEXIBILITY at $33.01/JP) and falls out of the top 8, replaced by
PersonWithTextOverlay.
