Ad Copy Guidelines

Core rules
	•	Upwork is a platform, not a staffing agency. It helps connect freelancers and clients; it does not vet, verify, or provide talent or projects, and it does not do the work.
	•	Never imply Upwork employs freelancers or guarantees any result.
	•	Avoid overselling, puffery, and unsubstantiated claims. All claims must be supported by data or proof assets.
	•	Payment protection is a perk, not a guarantee. Frame it accordingly.
Describing freelancers
Use “freelancers”, “freelancer professionals”, "freelancers on Upwork," "talent on Upwork," or "independent professionals." Never "Upwork freelancers," "Upwork's freelancers," or "Upwork talent."
Describing what Upwork does
Don't say
Say instead
"Upwork will find the right talent for you."
"Upwork's platform will help you find talent."
