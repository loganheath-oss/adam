TONE OF VOICE  
Upwork Tone of Voice for Paid Ads 
1) Voice to embody in ads 
●​ Clear & concise — plain words, specific, get to the point fast.​
 
●​ Supportive & perceptive — acknowledge needs, encourage with 
practical language.​
 
●​ Calm, collected, guiding — steer without pressure, offer direction and 
reassurance.​
 
●​ Professional — warm and human, never flippant or pushy.​
 
●​ Tone should feel: direct, supportive, considerate, grounded, truthful — 
with an occasional wink.​
 Never: wordy, robotic, overpromising, irreverent.​
 
 
2) Audience  
●​ Clients (SMB): Direct, actionable, confident, clear, supportive; 
sometimes light levity and fun. 
 
3) Funnel guidance 
●​ Awareness (prospecting): Hook, clarity, connection.​
 
●​ Consideration (retargeting): Clarity + proof (ratings, verified history).  
 
4) Headlines & hooks 
●​ 95/5 rule: 95% informative/helpful, 5% subtle personality (“wink”).​
 
●​ General: “Your shortcut to standout hires”​
 
●​ Copilot: “Work smart, not solo with Upwork”​
 
●​ AI × Talent: “Advanced AI. Human touch. Exceptional outcomes.”​
 
●​ Talent: “Finally, hiring that doesn’t feel like work”​
 
●​ Impact: “From ideas to outcomes — fast”​
 
 
5) Offer framing & proof 
●​ Emphasize ease & speed: post, review, hire fast.​
 
●​ Surface trust: ratings, verified history. 
●​ Suggest cost effectiveness without sounding like we’re cheap or low 
quality.​
 
●​ Clarity first, entertainment second.​
 
 
6) Terminology & naming 
●​ Use: client (hirer), freelancer/independent contractor/freelance 
professional (doer). Talent is singular.​
 
●​ Uma, Upwork’s Mindful AI™ (first mention). Then “Uma™” or “Uma” if 
context is clear.​
 
●​ AI always uppercase; “artificial intelligence” lowercase.​
 
●​ Avoid: click/tap/swipe/press, directional UI (above/below), escrow (say 
project funds).​
 
●​ Follow spelling list: email, website, WiFi, homepage, etc.​
 
 
7) Emojis & punctuation 
●​ Use sparingly in on-image copy when they make it punchy or fun. Use 
emojis in description copy in bulleted lists instead of bullet points. ​
​
 
●​ Keep punctuation simple & consistent for scannability.​
 
 
8) On-brand CTAs 
●​ Always verb-led, device-agnostic: Learn more, Find talent, Post a job, 
Start free.​
 
●​ Avoid “Click here.”​
 
●​ Focus on what the user can do.​
 
 
9) Quick ad-copy checklist 
●​ Clarity in line 1: payoff obvious, short, scannable.​
 
●​ Tone: supportive, confident, grounded; subtle wink optional.​
 
●​ Audience fit: speak to small businesses​
 
●​ Proof: include ratings/verified history when possible.​
 
●​ Terminology: correct brand naming, Uma™ handled properly.​
 
●​ CTA: single, clear, user-action-led.​
 
●​ Compliance: no overpromising; professional & respectful.​
 
 
10) Starter ad-ready lines 
●​ Prospecting:​
 
○​ “Finally, hiring that doesn’t feel like work”​
 
○​ “Work smart, not solo with Upwork”​
 
○​ “Advanced AI. Human touch. Exceptional outcomes”​
 
●​ Retargeting:​
 
○​ “Guiding you to go-to talent, fast”​
 
○​ “From ideas to outcomes — fast”
