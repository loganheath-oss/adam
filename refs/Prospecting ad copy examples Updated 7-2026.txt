How to use this document
This is a copy-only reference bank. Use it to calibrate tone, structure, and creative range when writing new prospecting ad copy. Each entry contains only the five fields relevant to copy output:
* Primary_Text_Short — ≤125 characters
* Primary_Text_Long — ≤300 characters
* Text_On_Visual — copy printed on the creative
* Headline_Short — ≤30 characters
* Headline_Long — ≤50 characters
Short and long versions are grouped together per ad. Where only one variant existed in the source, it is listed as a single example.
