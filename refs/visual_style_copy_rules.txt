UPWORK PAID ACQUISITION — PER-VISUAL-STYLE COPY RULES
======================================================

These rules define how ad copy should differ based on the visual style ordered.
The copy generator reads these rules and adapts headline length, body format,
CTA placement, and tone based on which style is being produced.

General rules that apply to ALL styles:
- Follow the Upwork brand voice: clear, concise, supportive, professional
- 95/5 rule: 95% informative/helpful, 5% subtle personality
- Use only approved claims and verified statistics
- Never overpromise or use superlatives without sourced proof
- CTA should always be actionable and specific


1. GRAPHIC WITH TEXT
   Copy role: Primary. Text carries the message alongside an illustrated graphic.
   Headline: 5-8 words. Clear, direct statement.
   Body: 1-2 sentences max. Supports the headline with one proof point.
   CTA: Action verb + benefit. "Post your first job" not "Learn more"
   Tone: Confident, modern.
   Avoid: Long paragraphs. The graphic does half the communication work.

2. SPLIT SCREEN
   Copy role: Shared with visual. Two sides create a comparison or contrast.
   Headline: Short phrase for each side. "Before Upwork / After Upwork" structure.
   Body: Minimal or none. The contrast tells the story.
   CTA: Single CTA below or between the two sides.
   Tone: Punchy, contrast-driven.
   Avoid: Explaining the comparison in words. The visual handles it.

3. US VS THEM
   Copy role: Comparative. Two opposing positions.
   Headline: Frame the comparison clearly. "Old way vs Upwork way"
   Body: 1 bullet or line per side. Keep it scannable.
   CTA: Positions Upwork as the obvious choice.
   Tone: Confident but not aggressive. Never name competitors directly.
   Avoid: Being mean-spirited. Show why Upwork is better, don't tear others down.

4. PHOTO WITH TEXT (UPWORK SHELL)
   Copy role: Supporting. Photo is the hero, text reinforces.
   Headline: 4-6 words. Complements the photograph.
   Body: 1 sentence. Context for what the photo shows.
   CTA: Standard Upwork CTA.
   Tone: Warm, human, grounded.
   Avoid: Text that competes with the photo for attention.

5. LIFESTYLE PHOTO (FULL BLEED)
   Copy role: Overlay. Text sits on top of a full-bleed photograph.
   Headline: 3-5 words. Must be readable against any background.
   Body: None or 1 short line. Minimal text — the image is the story.
   CTA: Bold, visible, high contrast.
   Tone: Aspirational but grounded. Show the outcome, not the process.
   Avoid: Long text. Anything that blocks the photograph.

6. TESTIMONIAL
   Copy role: Primary. The user quote IS the ad.
   Headline: The testimonial quote itself. Use real quotes only.
   Body: Attribution — name, role, company (if approved).
   CTA: Soft. "See more stories" or "Join [name] on Upwork"
   Tone: Authentic, human. The user's voice, not Upwork's.
   Avoid: Editing quotes to sound like marketing copy. Keep them real.

7. SOCIAL MEDIA PROFILE
   Copy role: Minimal. The mockup of a social profile does the work.
   Headline: Profile name or tagline shown within the mockup.
   Body: None outside the mockup. Everything lives in the UI.
   CTA: "Find pros like [name]" or "Browse talent"
   Tone: Aspirational — this is the kind of talent you can hire.
   Avoid: Text outside the mockup that duplicates what's inside it.

8. PIE CHART
   Copy role: Data-driven. The chart visualizes a stat.
   Headline: State the insight the chart reveals. "72% of contracts completed"
   Body: 1 sentence contextualizing the data point.
   CTA: Connects the stat to action. "See what gets done on Upwork"
   Tone: Factual, authoritative. Let the numbers speak.
   Avoid: Vague data. Every number must come from the approved claims doc.

9. HYBRID
   Copy role: Balanced. Both photography and UI elements share space.
   Headline: 5-7 words. Bridges the real-world photo and the digital UI.
   Body: 1-2 sentences. Explain how the platform enables the real-world outcome.
   CTA: Standard action CTA.
   Tone: Modern, capable, trustworthy.
   Avoid: Cluttered messaging. The layout is already complex — keep copy clean.

10. SEARCH RESULTS
    Copy role: Contextual. The search bar UI shows results.
    Headline: The search query itself becomes the headline. "web developer react"
    Body: None or minimal. The search results tell the story.
    CTA: "Start searching" or "Find your match"
    Tone: Functional, direct. Like using the actual product.
    Avoid: Marketing language that breaks the UI illusion.

11. SEARCH BAR WITH TALENT BADGE
    Copy role: Minimal. Search bar + branded badge elements.
    Headline: The search query. Keep it realistic.
    Body: Badge text — "Top Rated" "100% Job Success" "Expert-Vetted"
    CTA: "Search Upwork" or "Find talent"
    Tone: Product-focused. This is showing the tool.
    Avoid: Copy that doesn't belong in a search UI.

12. TEXT ONLY
    Copy role: Everything. Text IS the ad.
    Headline: 5-10 words. Must be compelling enough to stand alone.
    Body: 2-3 sentences. This is where you make the full argument.
    CTA: Bold, clear, actionable.
    Tone: Direct, confident. Every word earns its place.
    Avoid: Filler. With no visual support, weak copy is immediately obvious.

13. CHAT BUBBLE
    Copy role: Conversational. Appears as a text message or chat thread.
    Headline: The message content. Written like a real person texting.
    Body: 1-2 chat bubbles. Keep it natural.
    CTA: "Start the conversation" or "Message a freelancer"
    Tone: Casual, human, authentic. This should feel like real communication.
    Avoid: Marketing speak inside a chat bubble. Nobody texts like that.

14. NOTIFICATION
    Copy role: Alert-style. Appears as a mobile app notification.
    Headline: Notification title. Short, actionable. "New proposal received"
    Body: Notification preview text. 1 line.
    CTA: Implied by the notification. "Open Upwork" or "View proposals"
    Tone: Functional, urgent but not alarming. Like a real notification.
    Avoid: Long text. Notifications are short by nature.

15. REMINDER
    Copy role: Nudge. Mobile app reminder format.
    Headline: Reminder title. "Your project is waiting"
    Body: 1 line of context. "3 proposals ready to review"
    CTA: Implied. "Open" or "Review now"
    Tone: Helpful, timely. Not pushy.
    Avoid: Aggressive urgency. Reminders should feel supportive.

16. DEVICE UI (PHOTO)
    Copy role: Minimal. The Upwork UI shown on a device (laptop/phone) is the hero.
    Headline: 3-5 words about what the UI shows. "Your talent pipeline, organized"
    Body: None or 1 sentence. The screenshot speaks for itself.
    CTA: "Try it yourself" or "See it in action"
    Tone: Product-forward. Show, don't tell.
    Avoid: Describing what people can already see in the screenshot.

17. PLATFORM UI
    Copy role: Contextual. A highlighted moment from the Upwork interface.
    Headline: Name the feature or moment. "Instant AI matching"
    Body: 1 sentence explaining what the user is seeing.
    CTA: Feature-specific. "Try Copilot" or "Post a job"
    Tone: Modern, capable. Highlight the innovation.
    Avoid: Generic headlines that don't connect to the specific UI shown.

18. MEME
    Copy role: The joke. Humor is the hook.
    Headline: The meme text. Must be genuinely funny AND on-brand.
    Body: None. Memes don't need body copy.
    CTA: Light touch. "Been there? Upwork can help" or similar.
    Tone: Relatable, self-aware. The 5% personality dialed up.
    Avoid: Forced humor. Inside jokes. Anything that could alienate.

19. STICKY NOTE
    Copy role: Handwritten feel. Key message on a sticky note.
    Headline: Written as if someone jotted it on a real sticky note.
    Body: None outside the note.
    CTA: Casual. "We can help with that"
    Tone: Human, relatable, slightly informal.
    Avoid: Perfect marketing copy. Sticky notes should feel spontaneous.

20. POLL
    Copy role: Interactive feel. Poll format shows multiple options.
    Headline: The poll question. "How do you find freelancers?"
    Body: Poll options. 3-4 choices with Upwork as the clear winner.
    CTA: "Join the majority" or "Try the top choice"
    Tone: Social, engaging. Feels like participating.
    Avoid: Rigging the poll too obviously. Keep it clever.

21. TWEET / POST MOCKUP
    Copy role: Social proof. Looks like a real social media post.
    Headline: The tweet/post content. Written in social media voice.
    Body: Engagement metrics if relevant (likes, retweets).
    CTA: "See why people are talking" or "Join the conversation"
    Tone: Authentic social media voice. Not corporate.
    Avoid: Making it look fake. Should feel like a real post someone would write.

22. TEXT WITH BUTTON AND CURSOR
    Copy role: Action-oriented. Headline + UI button + cursor pointer.
    Headline: 4-6 words. Direct statement.
    Body: The button text IS the CTA. "Get started" "Post a job" "Find talent"
    CTA: The button itself. Make it the focal point.
    Tone: Clean, direct, action-focused.
    Avoid: Competing text. The button should be the obvious next step.

23. TALENT PROFILE
    Copy role: Showcase. Platform talent profile(s) with key messaging.
    Headline: About the talent category. "AI Engineers, ready now"
    Body: Profile details — skills, rating, job success rate.
    CTA: "Browse talent" or "Hire [category]"
    Tone: Impressive but authentic. Real profiles, real credentials.
    Avoid: Generic profiles. Use specific, believable specializations.

24. BESPOKE
    Copy role: Varies. Newly concepted layout.
    Headline: Adapt to the specific concept. No default rules.
    Body: As needed by the concept.
    CTA: Must still follow Upwork CTA guidelines.
    Tone: On-brand regardless of format.
    Note: Bespoke concepts should still follow all brand voice and compliance rules.


============================================================================
STRUCTURAL COPY LIMITS — by visual style
============================================================================
These are the hard character ceilings enforced by Brandon's templates. The
copy generator MUST clip or regenerate if a field exceeds its max. Source:
template frame annotations in Figma (Adtype: <style> > Copy Rules), captured
2026-05-11.

TEXT ONLY
  headline       max 104   (template layer: TextOnly_headline_text — "Quote")
  subhead        max 52    (template layer: TextOnly_Subhead_Text   — "Name")
  cta            max 14    (template layer: cta_text)

SPLIT SCREEN
  headline       max 36    (template layer: SplitScreen_Headline_Text)
  cta            max 14    (template layer: cta_text)
  (No subhead. Two image slots — left_image_placeholder, right_image_placeholder.)

REMINDER
  label          max 52    (static "Reminder" — usually not regenerated)
  body           max 104   (template layer: Notification_Headline_Text — "Quote")
  cta            max 32    (template layer: Notification_SubjectName_Text — soft limit, no Brandon spec)

MEME
  caption        max 104   (template layer: SplitScreen_Headline_Text — "Quote")
  cta            (none — Cta Pill is hidden in Brandon's design; do not generate)
  image          (none — meme image is BAKED INTO the template, one meme per template family)
  note: Each meme template (this-is-fine, future Drake, Distracted Boyfriend,
        etc.) is a separate template family. Pipeline picks the family by
        order-time meme selection (once more memes exist); copy generator
        writes a caption appropriate for THAT specific meme. The meme image
        itself is never overwritten by the pipeline.
