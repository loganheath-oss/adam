What ADAM is
An internal, AI-powered creative pipeline that transforms how Paid Acquisition creative is concepted, produced, scaled, 
and optimized.
A creative operating system — not a one-click generator — designed to turn performance signals into high-quality, on-brand 
assets at speed.
What ADAM does
The system enables the team to:
●
Turn performance insights into clear creative direction
●
Rapidly generate on-brief concepts and variations
●
Produce channel-native assets at scale (static, copy, and motion)
●
Enforce brand, voice, and legal guardrails automatically
●
Continuously learn from live performance to improve outputs

In its mature state:
●
ADAM should be able to generate and iterate paid social statics, modular copy systems 
(headlines, primaries, CTAs), and light motion and MOGRT components for long-form motion 
outputs for all of our social and paid channels at scale.
●
Performance metrics will be gathered by an AI agent to share with ADAM’s order form.
What ADAM ultimately makes
Initial focus is on static-ﬁrst, with controlled expansion into motion. (Aiming for May 1.)
Phase 1:
●
ADAM will generate and iterate paid social statics and modular copy systems (headlines, 
primaries, CTAs) for Meta ads at scale. 
●
At this stage, performance metrics will be added to ADAM manually to inform each batch 
of creative. 
22
© 2025 Upwork Inc. Proprietary and conﬁdential. Do not distribute.
ADAM won’t:
●
Generate long-form video assets (AI capabilities are not there yet.)
●
Generate completed motion assets (It will create motion components that will be 
refined by the graphic designer.)
●
Automatically pull performance data and “learn” from it in a continuous 
production loop (ADAM will receive performance insights as a variable to consider — not as 
training instructions.) 
Why it matters: Continuous training on performance data can cause the model to drift away from Upwork's 
guidelines over time. This keeps ADAM anchored.
