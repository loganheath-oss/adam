Purpose 
The primary goal of this framework is to align our category marketing focus to drive three core outcomes: 
 
1.​ Demand Capture in proven, high-converting categories. 
2.​ Demand Expansion by increasing second-project starts within 60 days. 
3.​ Demand Creation in AI categories we want the brand to be known for. 
This framework guides where we invest across SEO, social search, paid acquisition, lifecycle marketing, product surfaces, and 
content. 
Category Framework 
Demand Capture 
Demand Capture categories are established areas where the marketplace consistently produces successful hiring outcomes at 
scale and we want more first-project acquisition at scale. 
 
●​ Meaningful organic demand 
●​ Strong conversion-to-CL starts 
●​ Clear, scoped first projects 
●​ Reliable fill rates 
 
These are the categories we confidently lead with at last touch points of acquisition (paid, SEO, cold outreach). We want these 
categories to feel modern, relevant, and scalable.
