CRITICAL RULE FROM BRIAN (CREATIVE DIRECTOR):

TEMPLATE A: LIFESTYLE PHOTO (FULL BLEED)
-----------------------------------------
Brandon's rules:
- Clear visual hierarchy with top-down reading order
- Human-centered or lifestyle imagery that reflects target users and use cases
- Framed at center. Image bleeds to the edge of design (full bleed, no border)
- White or black text, white or black CTA pill with white text
- CTA pill (container fill) and text must be same color
- Balanced spacing between text lines
- Immediate clarity of value proposition without extra explanation
- Logo at bottom right corner, aligned to bottom of CTA pill
- Padding around logo must match border thickness of overall design
- Headline + CTA positioned in bottom lower left
- Headline size varies by character count (more text = smaller font)
- Copy must not cover more than 40% of the design

From transcript:
- Gradient adapts (not fixed)
- 24 characters max for headline (can be exceeded but Brandon adjusts in proofing)
- Photos must come from approved brand library, not AI-generated
- Layout will auto-adjust to content via Figma component system


TEMPLATE B: PHOTO WITH TEXT (UPWORK SHELL)
-------------------------------------------
Brandon's rules:
- Clear visual hierarchy with top-down reading order
- Human-centered imagery that reflects target users and use cases
- Framed at center. Image bleeds to the edge of the internal border
- White borders, black text, black CTA pill with white text
- Balanced spacing and padding for visual clarity
- Rounded corners 37.33%
- Immediate clarity of value proposition without extra explanation
- Logo at top right corner, positioned at edge of corner
- Padding around logo must match border thickness
- Headline + CTA positioned in bottom lower left

From transcript:

- Subject should be center or slightly right so mask doesn't hide them

  "that ad type might not be the sexiest thing, but if we can nail that one,
  they would be very happy"
- Logo placement: always top right or bottom right

KEY INSIGHT FOR THE PIPELINE:
------------------------------
Brandon said: "The brand is so simple. We can just have all of the templates built out
pre-made in Figma. The only thing being replaced is the photography we're pulling from
our guidelines and the copy that's going to go in from the GPT. Everything else could
be pre-built."

This means:
1. Templates are FIXED designs — pipeline doesn't change the layout
2. Pipeline provides TWO things only: a photo from the brand library + generated copy
3. Figma components handle all the auto-adjustment (font sizing, shape adaptation)
4. No AI image generation for photo styles — only library selection
